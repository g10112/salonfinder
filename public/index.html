Your privacy

Favourites, personal notes and your preferred distance unit are stored only in this browser's local storage. Your location is requested only when you press “Near me”. Search terms and coordinates are sent to this app's server, then to Geoapify in live mode to find nearby places. This demo has no accounts, analytics, adverts or appointment system.

Use “Clear my saved data” below to delete browser-stored favourites and notes. Web hosting and Geoapify may process standard server requests under their own policies.
